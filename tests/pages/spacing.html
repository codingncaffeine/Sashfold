Letters and words

Plain text, no extra room anywhere.

Four px after every letter.

Two px taken back from every letter.

Twelve px after every space.

Two after letters and eight after words.

A quarter em at 24px.

Wide letters wrap sooner in a narrow box than they would without.

The underline reaches the last gap.

The first line, indented

Forty px in on the first line only, and the second line starts flush at the edge again.

A quarter of the block, on the first line only, and the rest flush.

Twenty px back out of the padding, hanging the first line to the left of the rest.

Centred within what the indent leaves.

Right-aligned within what the indent leaves.

Inherited into the block inside, whose own first line takes it.

The indent is measured from the edge the float leaves, and the lines beside it are not indented at all.
