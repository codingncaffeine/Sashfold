What a box draws and what it spaces

A filled box, and one that pads its text.

Padding above and below reaches past the line.

A box with a border all round it.

One thick on two sides only.

A box held apart by its margins.

A hairline box.

Boxes that carry on

A box whose text runs past the end of one line opens on the first and closes on the last.

Nested: outer inner outer again.

A bigger box takes the height its own font asks for.

An empty one, , is its padding alone.
